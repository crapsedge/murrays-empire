About Murrays Empire LLC

Murrays Empire LLC is an Omaha-based company focused on developing, operating, and expanding specialized service brands.

Through its operating brands, the company develops digital experiences, marketing systems, and referral processes designed to connect consumers with independent and participating service providers.

Our current portfolio includes Omaha Restoration™ and Smart Home Security, with additional service-brand opportunities evaluated as the company continues to grow.
